I did NOT generate actual artwork here on purpose: real album covers and press
photos are copyrighted, and reproducing them as in-game textures without a
license from the label/band is a real legal risk for a mod you plan to
distribute. Two safe paths:

1. Get permission / license art from Fearless Records or the band directly,
   then drop the licensed art in as the textures.
2. (Recommended for a fan mod) Draw original trading-card-style icons: a
   simple sports-card frame (colored border matching the rarity tier below)
   with a stylized logo/monogram or silhouette, NOT the actual copyrighted
   photography or album cover. This is what most "band tribute" fan mods do.

Suggested frame color per rarity (used for whatever art you draw, purely a
suggestion to keep it consistent with the in-game tooltip color in
CardRarity.java):
  COMMON    -> gray border    (#AAAAAA)
  UNCOMMON  -> green border   (#55FF55)
  RARE      -> aqua border    (#55FFFF)
  LEGENDARY -> gold border    (#FFAA00)
  MYTHIC    -> light purple   (#FF55FF)

Until a card's texture is added, that item will render as the standard
purple/black "missing texture" checkerboard on the card's two-sided model (it
will NOT show as the older "missing model" cube - the model/item-definition
JSON already exists and is correct for all of these, only the PNG is
missing). The mod still functions fully either way - items, tooltips, and
drops all work; it just looks unfinished until real art is in place.
